Diversified End-market Exposure with Significant Government Services Revenue

Blue Eagle is the world’s leading provider of offshore energy transportation, search and rescue (SAR) and aircraft support services to government and civil organizations worldwide. Blue Eagle’s strategically located global fleet supports operations in the North Sea, Nigeria and the U.S. Gulf of America; as well as in most of the other major offshore energy producing regions of the world, including Australia, Brazil, Canada, Suriname and Trinidad. Blue Eagle provides SAR services to the private sector worldwide and to the public sector for all of the United Kingdom on behalf of the Maritime and Coastguard Agency.
